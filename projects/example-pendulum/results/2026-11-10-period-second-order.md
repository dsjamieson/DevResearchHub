# Period correction to $O(\theta_0^2)$

> **Superseded** by [the fourth-order result](2026-11-17-period-fourth-order.md). Kept for the record.

## Result

$T/T_0 = 1 + \theta_0^2/16$. At $\theta_0 = 0.5$ this gives $1.015625$, while the exact value is $1.015\,853$ (relative error $2.2\times10^{-4}$).

Analytic; `code` and `data` are n/a because nothing was computed numerically.
